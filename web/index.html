the rule

Two questions decide everything.

Does its identity bind the bytes?
If the hash is of the canonical form, equal facts are one fact. Drift becomes a type error, not a silent duplicate.
Can a stranger re-derive it?
If the projection carries its generator and witness, correctness is checked — not vouched for. Reputation drops out.

Everything above runs from one library. The CLI emits these exact numbers as JSON; this page and the slide deck → render them. Nothing here is mocked.

Baseline. The three failures above are catalogued in P. Song, P. Han & N. Goodman, “Large Language Model Reasoning Failures,” arXiv:2602.06176 (2026). canon.d doesn't fix the model — it puts the fact on a substrate where these failures can't survive, then proves it against a real measurement.
